# 🌱 Darukaa.Earth — Biodiversity Intelligence System

### AI-Powered Environmental Reasoning & Evidence Platform

Darukaa.Earth is an AI-powered biodiversity intelligence system that analyzes interconnected environmental conditions across **soil, climate, land use, habitat, and biodiversity**.

Instead of analyzing environmental variables independently, the system combines **multi-metric reasoning, an environmental knowledge graph, and scientific evidence retrieval** to generate explainable and actionable environmental recommendations.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Environmental conditions are strongly interconnected.

For example:

- Low soil organic carbon can affect soil functions and moisture-related processes.
- Low rainfall can increase water availability pressure.
- Monoculture can reduce vegetation and habitat diversity.
- Habitat fragmentation can reduce habitat connectivity and species movement.
- These pressures can influence biodiversity indicators such as species richness and habitat diversity.

Traditional environmental analysis may examine these variables separately.

**Darukaa.Earth connects multiple environmental factors and explains the relationships between them.**

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of Darukaa.Earth is to build a conversational environmental intelligence system that can:

1. Understand natural-language environmental information.
2. Extract structured environmental metrics.
3. Identify environmental pressures.
4. Analyze relationships between multiple environmental variables.
5. Retrieve relevant scientific evidence.
6. Generate actionable recommendations.
7. Explain why each recommendation is relevant.
8. Identify environmental metrics that may be affected.
